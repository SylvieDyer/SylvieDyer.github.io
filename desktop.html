× Photography
A picture is worth a thousand words...

One of my favorite hobbies is taking photos of the world around me! In this app you can see some of the places I've been to as seen through my eyes... or, rather, through the lens of my Cannon Rebel T5i ;)

I love exploring new places, and I hope to travel more in the coming years. Some of my bucket list travel destinations are Iceland, Alberta (Canada), the fjords of Norway, and New Zealand, to name a few. *Note that some of these locations are slightly more refined than the others...

These photos are a collection of some of my favorite places, wildest adventures, and the places I've called home over the years. I won't bore you with the backstory for each of them, but I hope you enjoy looking through this collection of photos that I've compiled over the past decade :)
